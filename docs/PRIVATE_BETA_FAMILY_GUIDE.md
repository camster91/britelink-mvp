# BriteLink Private Beta Family Guide

**Welcome to the BriteLink private beta.** This guide will help you understand what to expect, how to use the service, and how to provide feedback that helps us deliver the best possible homeschool curriculum planning experience.

## What is BriteLink?

BriteLink is a professional homeschool curriculum planning and support service. Unlike AI tutoring apps, BriteLink pairs you with a **real credentialed educator** who designs a personalized weekly learning plan for your child.

### What Makes BriteLink Different

**Human expertise, not algorithms:**
- A credentialed educator reviews your intake and creates a custom 8-week curriculum
- Every plan is independently reviewed by a second educator before delivery
- You get a named educator contact for questions and revision requests

**Parent-guided learning:**
- You receive clear instructions, materials lists, and adaptations
- Your child learns with your guidance—this is not passive screen time
- All resources are educator-verified and age-appropriate

**Trust and transparency:**
- Honest delivery timelines (5-7 business days for new plans)
- Secure case-specific messaging—your family's information stays private
- Full data export, correction, and deletion rights

## Getting Started

### 1. Account Access

You will receive an email invitation to create your BriteLink account:

1. Click the magic link in the invitation email
2. You'll be signed in automatically—no password needed
3. Bookmark the BriteLink URL for future access

**Important:** 
- Each sign-in requires a new magic link sent to your email
- Links expire after 1 hour for security
- Only invited email addresses can access BriteLink

### 2. Complete Intake

Your first task is to complete the intake form. This is how your educator learns about your child's learning needs.

**The intake has three parts:**

**Part 1: Learning Context (6 fields)**
- Your child's age and current grade level
- Learning objectives (what you want them to learn)
- Subjects to include
- Preferred learning style
- Current curriculum (if any)
- Additional context

**Part 2: Household Setup (4 fields)**
- Available learning time per day
- Available resources (books, internet, manipulatives)
- Learning environment description
- Technical access

**Part 3: Consent (2 required checkboxes)**
- Privacy notice acknowledgment
- Terms of service agreement

**Tips for a great intake:**
- Be specific about learning objectives (e.g., "Master multiplication facts 1-12" vs "Get better at math")
- Mention any learning differences, accommodations, or special interests
- Describe your available time honestly—your plan will be designed for what's realistic
- List resources you already have so your educator can incorporate them

**Time required:** 15-30 minutes

### 3. Wait for Plan Delivery

After you submit intake:

1. Your case enters the educator queue
2. An educator reviews your intake for clarity
3. If anything is unclear, they'll send a clarification message
4. Your educator designs your 8-week curriculum
5. A second educator independently reviews the plan
6. You receive a delivery notification

**Timeline:** 5-7 business days (beta may be slightly longer)

**During this time:**
- Check your case messages for any clarification requests
- You can update your learner profile if you think of additional context
- No action needed—we'll notify you when your plan is ready

### 4. Review Your Learning Plan

When your plan is delivered, you'll see:

**8-week overview:**
- Each week's focus and objectives
- Estimated time commitment
- Required and optional resources

**Daily lessons:**
- Clear learning objective
- Step-by-step instructions
- Materials needed
- Adaptations for different learning styles or needs
- Estimate of adult help required
- Educator-approved resources (websites, videos, printables)

**How to read a lesson:**
1. Read the objective—this is what your child should be able to do by the end
2. Gather materials before you start
3. Follow instructions in order
4. Use adaptations if the main approach isn't working
5. Check off the lesson when complete

### 5. Execute the Plan

**Daily routine:**
1. Open BriteLink and navigate to Learning Plan
2. Select the current week and day
3. Review today's lesson(s)
4. Gather materials
5. Guide your child through the lesson
6. Mark the lesson complete, in progress, or skipped
7. Add a caregiver note if helpful (optional)

**Activity tracking:**
- **Complete:** Lesson finished as planned
- **In Progress:** Started but not finished (will resume later)
- **Skipped:** Chose not to do this lesson (not rescheduled)
- **Rescheduled:** Moving this lesson to a future date

**Rescheduling:**
- If you skip a day, you can reschedule incomplete lessons
- Select the new target date
- Optionally add a note explaining the change
- Your data is never lost—you can always come back to a lesson

**Caregiver notes:**
- Private notes about how the lesson went
- What worked, what was challenging
- Your child's reaction or interest level
- Helpful for revision requests later

### 6. Message Your Educator

You have secure case-specific messaging for:
- Questions about a lesson or resource
- Requesting clarification on instructions
- Sharing how the plan is going
- Reporting broken links or inappropriate content

**How to send a message:**
1. Navigate to Educator Support
2. Write your message (be specific!)
3. Optionally attach a file (work sample, screenshot, etc.)
4. Click Send

**Response time:** Within 1 business day (beta target)

**Tips for effective messages:**
- Reference the specific week and lesson if applicable
- Describe what you tried before messaging
- Attach examples if helpful (your child's work, a screenshot of an error)
- Be clear about whether you need an urgent response

**Important:** 
- Messages are case-scoped and private to your household
- Do not include sensitive medical or legal information
- For urgent safety concerns, contact [support contact] directly

### 7. Request a Revision

If the plan isn't working after trying it for 1-2 weeks, you can request a revision.

**Revisions are for:**
- Lessons are too easy or too hard
- Time commitment is unrealistic
- Learning style mismatch
- Missing subjects or objectives
- Resources aren't accessible

**Revisions are NOT for:**
- Day-to-day schedule changes (use rescheduling instead)
- Minor tweaks (message your educator first)
- Immediate turnaround (revisions follow the same 5-7 day process)

**Each package includes:**
- Essentials: 0 revisions (messaging support only)
- Complete: 1 revision
- Annual: 4 revisions

**How to request a revision:**
1. Navigate to Educator Support
2. Click "Request Revision"
3. Describe what's not working and what you need
4. Submit the request

**What happens next:**
1. Your educator reviews your request and activity data
2. They design a revised plan addressing your concerns
3. The revision is independently reviewed
4. You receive the updated plan (5-7 business days)

**Tips for revision requests:**
- Try the plan for at least 1-2 weeks before requesting a revision
- Be specific about what's not working
- Provide examples from your caregiver notes
- Explain your desired outcome

## Privacy and Data

### What Data We Collect

**Required for service delivery:**
- Intake information (child's age, grade, learning objectives, etc.)
- Lesson activity (completion status, reschedule dates, caregiver notes)
- Case messages and attachments
- Plan delivery acknowledgments

**Not collected:**
- Your child's full legal name (first name only)
- Social Security Number or government ID
- Medical diagnoses (general descriptions only)
- School records or IEP documents

### How We Use Your Data

**Your data is used to:**
- Design and deliver your personalized learning plan
- Improve plan quality through educator review
- Respond to your messages and revision requests
- Fulfill your privacy requests (export, correction, deletion)

**Your data is NEVER:**
- Sold to third parties
- Shared with other families
- Used for marketing to other people
- Shared across households (even by accident—we have technical safeguards)

### Your Privacy Rights

**You can:**
- **Export** your complete case data anytime (JSON format with all attachments)
- **Correct** your learner profile information
- **Request deletion** of your household and all associated data
- **Withdraw consent** and stop service

**How to exercise rights:**
1. Navigate to Privacy & Data
2. Choose Export, Correction, or Deletion
3. For export/deletion, you'll need to sign in again (recent authentication required)
4. Follow the prompts

**Deletion timeline:**
- Request is reviewed within 5 business days
- Data is scheduled for permanent deletion
- 30-day grace period for accidental requests
- After 30 days, all data is permanently erased

### Data Security

**We protect your data by:**
- Household isolation: You can only see your own family's data
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
- Regular security audits
- Minimal data collection
- Educator-only access to your case (not visible to other families)

**If something goes wrong:**
- We'll notify you within 72 hours of discovering a privacy breach
- We'll explain what happened and what we're doing about it
- We'll help you protect your family

## Troubleshooting

### I didn't receive my magic link email

1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Verify you're using the email address you were invited with
3. Wait 5 minutes—sometimes emails are delayed
4. Contact [support contact] if still not received

### I can't see my learning plan

**If intake is incomplete:**
- Complete all three parts of intake
- Consent checkboxes are required

**If intake is complete but no plan yet:**
- Check "Educator Support" for clarification messages
- Normal delivery is 5-7 business days
- You'll receive a notification when delivered

**If plan was delivered but not visible:**
- Refresh your browser
- Try signing out and back in
- Contact [support contact] if still not visible

### A resource link is broken

1. Note the week, day, and lesson
2. Send a message to your educator with the broken link
3. They'll provide an alternative resource
4. Broken links are fixed in the next revision

### I need to change my child's profile

1. Navigate to Learner Profile
2. Update the information
3. Click Save
4. Your educator will see the updated information

**Note:** Changing profile information doesn't automatically trigger a plan revision. If the change is significant (e.g., your child's learning needs have changed dramatically), request a revision.

### I accidentally deleted a caregiver note

Unfortunately, deleted notes cannot be recovered. However:
- Lesson activity data (complete/skipped/rescheduled) is preserved
- You can add a new note
- Your educator doesn't see individual notes unless you share them in a message

### The app is slow or not loading

1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh your browser (Cmd+R or Ctrl+R)
3. Clear your browser cache
4. Try a different browser
5. Contact [support contact] if the problem persists

### I see someone else's data

**This is a critical privacy issue. Do not interact with the data.**

1. Do NOT read, copy, or share the information
2. Take a screenshot (without sensitive details visible)
3. Contact [support contact] IMMEDIATELY
4. Note the date and time
5. Sign out and do not sign back in until you hear from us

## Appendix: Beta Testing Best Practices

### How to Be a Great Beta Tester

**1. Use it realistically**
- Don't test edge cases exclusively
- Don't try to break the app
- Use it as you would if you were a paying customer

**2. Document problems clearly**
- What did you expect to happen?
- What actually happened?
- Can you reproduce it?
- Include screenshots if helpful

**3. Provide context**
- "This lesson was too hard" → "Week 2, Day 3: My 7-year-old couldn't sound out the vocabulary words, needed 45 minutes of help"
- "App is confusing" → "I couldn't find where to send a message. I looked in Settings and Learner Profile before finding it in Educator Support"

**4. Differentiate bugs from design choices**
- Bug: Something is broken (link doesn't work, page won't load, data is lost)
- Design feedback: Something works but could be better (button is hard to find, instructions are unclear, too many clicks)

**5. Be honest but kind**
- We want honest feedback, including criticism
- Frame feedback constructively
- Assume good intent—we're trying to build something great

### What We're NOT Testing

**Out of scope for this beta:**
- Multiple learners (coming later)
- Multi-language support (English only for now)
- Mobile native apps (responsive web works on phones)
- Real-time AI tutoring (intentionally not our model)
- Gamification (intentionally excluded)

If you need these features, please let us know—but they won't be built during this beta.

### Privacy During Testing

- Invite other families without our approval
- Share login credentials or magic links
- Test with someone else's child without consent
